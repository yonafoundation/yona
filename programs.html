What We Do

Our Programs & Services

From emergency food support to long-term empowerment — our programs are designed to meet people where they are.

🍱
Program 01

Food Support Program

Our flagship program provides weekly food packs and hot meals to families and individuals experiencing food insecurity. No ID required. No judgment. Just food and care.

  • Weekly food drive every Saturday 9am–1pm
  • Emergency meal packs for crisis situations
  • Nutritious groceries for families with children
  • Special dietary needs accommodated

Request Food Support
🏠
Program 02

Social Services & Referrals

We connect community members to essential services they may not know exist — from housing assistance to healthcare access and government welfare programs.

  • Housing assistance referrals and guidance
  • Healthcare and clinic access support
  • Benefits enrollment assistance
  • Utility and rent aid connections

Get Connected

Program 03

Emergency Aid Kits

When disaster or crisis strikes, we respond fast. Our emergency aid kits include clothing, hygiene items, and household essentials for families in sudden need.

  • Hygiene and personal care items
  • Warm clothing and blankets
  • Household essential supplies
  • 24–48 hour response time

Request Aid

Program 04

Youth Empowerment

Young people deserve a chance to thrive. Our youth programs provide mentorship, academic support, and skills training to help them build a better future.

  • After-school tutoring and mentorship
  • Life skills and career workshops
  • School supply giveaways
  • Summer empowerment camps
