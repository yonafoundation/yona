Who We Are

A Community Built on Compassion

YONA Foundation — You Are Not Alone — was founded with a simple but powerful belief: every person deserves dignity, nourishment, and support, regardless of their circumstances.

We are a grassroots nonprofit organization dedicated to fighting food insecurity and providing critical social services to vulnerable families and individuals in our community. From emergency food packs to long-term empowerment programs, we walk alongside those who need it most.

Our name says it all. When life gets hard, we want everyone to know — you are not alone.
